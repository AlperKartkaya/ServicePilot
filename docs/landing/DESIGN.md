## Overview
Persuade / Experience, scoped only to static/index.html and landing.css/js. The root DESIGN.md remains the operational application's authority. User explicitly approved code-first: this is a rendered implementation, not a reference-image match claim. Dark material comes from the requested service-bay setting, not generic SaaS decoration.

## Colors
Mint means useful evidence and forward motion; amber means insufficient evidence; coral means the safety gate. State is also written in text. Ivory headings and muted steel descriptions sit on near-black; no gradient type or decorative glow.

## Typography
Self-hosted Manrope variable Latin + Turkish coverage. Hero 44–74px desktop (line-height 1.14), 34–60px mobile (line-height 1.16). The selected promise is “HER DURUMDA, HIZLA SIRADAKİ ADIMA”, composed in three lines; the smaller mobile floor keeps it readable at 320px. Chapter headings 30–54px. Body 12–19px, compact metadata floor 11px. Measurement is deliberately oversized and tabular. No monospace costume.

## Layout
Opening: large left promise and unbranded concept truck lower-right. The six-step story is a persistent visual stage beside short chapters, not feature cards. At ≤700px the chapter text occupies the top of the stage and the evidence fills the rest. At <800px height or reduced-motion preference, scenes become normal-flow reading sections. No custom scroll container or mandatory scroll snapping.

## Elevation & Depth
Only the physical note has an offset soft shadow. Signal bars, candidate table and circuit are flat data geometry. The truck is a generated product illustration, explicitly labelled as representative.

## Shapes
Small rectangular controls and paper corners. Circular arrow, signal dots and semantic toggle only. Thin separators structure the decision process; no nested cards.

## Components
Hero CTA starts the story, never opens the MVP. Chapter links are real anchors; active step is text and mint. Evidence candidate utilities and route are produced by the existing RAEP engine with fixture probabilities. Risk toggle starts an alternative initial note and uses the actual safety gate. Hover and focus are visible; Space activates the toggle. Details discloses today's scope versus product vision. The top-right and closing “Demoyu dene” links open demo/. The hero CTA still starts the scroll story; the footer offers a story restart.

Motion: one GSAP ticker drives Lenis; one scrubbed story timeline morphs the same evidence workspace. matchMedia tears down animations and restores normal reading when motion preference changes. Missing runtimes leave all content readable. No live progress or provider activity is invented.

## Do's and Don'ts
Keep the synthetic label, explain probabilities are not accuracy, and show the human responsibility boundary. Preserve app routes/data. Don't claim photo analysis, a completed Isuzu partnership, field savings or actual expert delivery. Don't turn this into a dashboard or link-led teaser.

Compact phone and laptop windows below 800px viewport height use the complete linear story rather than compressing evidence into an overlapping pinned stage. This is intentional progressive enhancement, not a separate navigation flow.
